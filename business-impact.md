# Business Impact Summary — MFA Enforcement
## This document supports Business & Product Stakeholders

## Stakeholders Impacted
- **IT / Identity Access Management (IAM) Teams :** Defined enrollment enforcement, exception handling, and escalation logic
- **Security / Risk Teams :** Layered controls, behavioral signals, and auditable access decisions
- **Business Leadership :** Lower account compromise risk without unnecessary disruption to productivity
- **End Users :** Reduced authentication friction for low-risk logins, with clear recovery and escalation paths

## Benefits
- Reduced account compromise risk
- Improved security posture and audit readiness
- Clearer, enforceable access standard

## User Experience Impact
- +1 step at login
- Initial enrollment time (typically a few minutes)
- Potential friction for users without smartphones (provide an alternate method)

## Support Considerations
- Expect increased tickets during week 1–2
- Most common issues:
  - Enrollment confusion
  - Device lost/replaced
  - MFA prompts not appearing
  - Time sync / code mismatch (if applicable)

## Communications Plan (simple)
- Announcement email (what + why + when)
- “How to enroll” quick guide (potentially with visuals)
- Reminder 24 hours before enforcement
- 1st Rollout Day: Day-of support message + help desk link
