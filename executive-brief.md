# Executive Brief — MFA Enforcement

## What’s changing
We are enforcing multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all user logins.

## Why this matters
MFA significantly reduces the risk of account takeover by adding a second verification step beyond a password.

## Business impact (high-level)
- **Risk Reduction:** Lower likelihood of unauthorized access and data exposure  
- **Operational Stability:** Fewer security incidents tied to compromised credentials  
- **Compliance Readiness:** Supports common security expectations (ex. strong access controls)

## What to expect
- Users will be prompted to enroll MFA if not already enrolled  
- Login will require a second step (authentication app / SMS / token depending on policy)  
- A small increase in help desk tickets may occur during initial rollout

## Decision points / asks
- Confirm rollout timeline and user groups (phased vs all-at-once)
- Approve support communications plan and training message
- Align on “break-glass” access for critical accounts
